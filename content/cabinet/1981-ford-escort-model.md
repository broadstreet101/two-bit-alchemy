# 1981 Ford Escort Model

## Artifact Description

This is a white 1981 Ford Escort model in a display case.

It matters because it is not just a small version of a car. It is a small version of a car Dada actually owned: the same year, make, model, and color.

That real car was totaled in an accident where Dada's face went through the windshield.

## Provenance / Context

## Story Draft

At first glance, this is a white 1981 Ford Escort model in a display case.

It is also a memory of the real car.

Dada owned the same year, make, model, and color. That car was totaled in an accident near the hospital where he worked. In the accident, his face went through the windshield.

A plastic surgeon from a larger city happened to be available and repaired the injuries.

Those are the facts currently approved for the story. The exact date of the accident, the name of the hospital, the larger city, and the surgeon's name have not been captured or approved for publication.

The model came later from Jon, one of Dada's best friends. Jon was also close with James, and he had a particular gift for finding strangely specific things that made sense only if you knew the person receiving them.

This was one of those things.

The model has traveled with Dada for years. It now has another life on the shelf: it serves as the display pedestal for the signed Tori Amos Camel cigarette pack.

That connection matters too. The model links the car, the accident, survival, Jon, James, the Tori Amos artifact, and a later friendship between Jon's daughter and Juliet, who became close despite living in different states.

The story is not complete yet, but the object has clearly earned a shelf.
